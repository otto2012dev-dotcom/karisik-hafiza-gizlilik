Privacy Policy — Memory Mix

Last updated: 02.10.2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile application Memory Mix (published in Turkish as Karışık Hafıza), developed by Cenk Alper Aktuç.

In short

Memory Mix does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app does not connect to the internet.

Data we collect

None.

The app never asks for your name, surname, e-mail address, phone number, date of birth, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. You do not need to create an account or sign in.

What is stored on your device

The app keeps four things in your device's own local storage.

Your settings: whether music and sound effects are on or off, their volume levels, and the language you chose — so the app starts the same way next time.

Your game scores:

  • the date of the game
  • the score you earned
  • the theme you played
  • the number of cards
  • the number of moves you made
  • whether you used a hint

A technical event log (so that the cause of a problem can be found if one occurs):

  • when the app was opened and which screens were shown
  • games starting and ending, hint use
  • error messages that occurred
  • screen size, system language and browser/system version

The log contains no name, account, location or anything else that identifies you. At most the last 300 events are kept; older ones are deleted automatically.

These records stay only on your device. They are not sent to us, to any server or to any third party. When you uninstall the app, these records are deleted with it.

If you want to report a problem, you can copy the log yourself from inside the app and send it to us; this is entirely your choice and never happens on its own.

Internet connection

Memory Mix does not need an internet connection to work and does not connect to any server. All images and sounds in the app come from inside the app itself.

Advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising. No ad network, analytics tool, crash reporting service or user tracking technology (cookies, advertising ID, pixels, etc.) is used.

Sharing with third parties

Since no data is collected, no data is shared. We do not sell, rent or transfer your data to anyone.

In-app purchases

There are no in-app purchases. The app is bought with a one-time payment; the payment is handled entirely by the app store (Google Play / App Store) and your payment details never reach us.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for users of all ages. Since no personal data is collected from any user — including children — no data belonging to children is collected either.

App permissions

The app does not request sensitive device permissions such as contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or storage.

Changes to this policy

If a feature that collects data is ever added to the app, this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date will change. A significant change will be noted in the release notes on the app store.
